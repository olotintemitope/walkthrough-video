Maya Chen

Product designer · Portland, Oregon

About

Maya designs calm, useful tools for teams doing thoughtful work. She is interested in the small choices that make complicated systems feel welcoming, and in building products that respect the attention of the people who use them.

Over the last decade she has worked with early-stage teams, public-interest organizations, and established companies. Her practice moves between research, interaction design, prototyping, and the patient work of helping a team decide what matters most.

Current focus

Right now Maya is shaping a collaborative research workspace. The team is exploring how notes, decisions, and source material can stay connected as a project grows, without making the interface feel like another inbox to manage.

Beyond the screen

Maya spends weekends on long walks, tends a small balcony garden, and keeps a notebook of neighborhood signs. She volunteers with a local repair cafe and is slowly learning to make a decent loaf of sourdough.

She believes good work comes from curiosity, generous collaboration, and leaving enough time to notice what people are actually telling you.
